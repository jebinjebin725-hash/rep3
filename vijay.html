vijay

Vijay is a popular Indian actor and playback singer who works predominantly in Tamil cinema. He has appeared in over 60 films and is known for his charismatic screen presence, dance skills, and philanthropic activities. Vijay has a massive fan following and is often referred to as "Thalapathy" by his fans. Some of his notable films include "Ghilli," "Thuppakki," "Mersal," and "Master."
